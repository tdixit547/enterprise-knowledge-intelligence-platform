## Becoming a Maintainer

Maintainers are chosen from active contributors who have demonstrated a strong understanding of the system's architecture, particularly the strict security and RBAC components. If you are interested in becoming a maintainer, please consistently review PRs, triage issues, and contribute high-quality code.
